Axion vs three.js

Both engines draw the same scene at 960 × 540, one at a time, on a fresh canvas, following the same camera path. Uncapped renders frame after frame and waits for the GPU to finish each one, so the numbers aren't capped by your monitor's refresh rate. Refresh-locked is what you'd actually see in a normal render loop. The light brightness is matched by formula, so the two images should look close but not identical.
