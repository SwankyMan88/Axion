# Third-party assets

## sponza.js — Crytek Sponza

Packed from the Khronos glTF-Sample-Models `2.0/Sponza` by `scripts/pack-sponza.mjs`
(geometry quantized, textures re-encoded as WebP; content otherwise unchanged).

- Model: Crytek Sponza by Frank Meinl, Crytek — based on the original Sponza by Marko Dabrovic.
- Fixes and redistribution: Morgan McGuire (McGuire Computer Graphics Archive).
- PBR textures: Alexandre Pestana.
- glTF conversion: The Khronos Group.

Licensed under the terms stated at
https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models/tree/main/2.0/Sponza
(CC BY 3.0 per Crytek's release). This notice must accompany the asset.

## pine-valley-1.js, pine-valley-2.js — Pine Valley

Built by `scripts/build-valley.mjs` and joined by `scripts/bundle-valley.mjs` from models and textures by [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com),
released under CC0 (public domain). Meshes were simplified into levels of detail, foliage
turned into cards and impostors, and textures re-encoded as WebP. The terrain, its layout
and the placement of every object are generated by `scripts/valley/map.mjs`.

Credit is not required by CC0, but thanks to the Poly Haven artists and supporters who make it possible.
